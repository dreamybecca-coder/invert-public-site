Privacy

This notice covers the public website and the minimum remote account data used for invited INVERT App testing.

Updated August 1, 2026

Who operates this website

INVERT is operated by Rebecca as an independent developer. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to privacy@invertagent.com.

Public website data

The public site does not currently use analytics, advertising pixels, session replay or marketing cookies. The hosting provider may process short-lived technical logs such as IP address, request time, requested page, browser information and error data to deliver and secure the site.

Invited account data

When invited account testing applies, the remote account layer may process an account identifier, verified email address, an optional and unverified phone number, invitation redemption, session, security and consent records. Supabase provides account and database infrastructure; Resend provides transactional email delivery.

Signing in does not automatically upload Room content, memory, Vault files, model-provider keys or other local investment work. Product analytics and full conversation or research-content collection are not enabled in the initial account scope.

If you email us, we receive the address, message and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond and maintain necessary records.

External services

Links to Substack, X and other services take you to their websites. Their privacy practices apply there. The public INVERT site does not receive your activity on those services unless you separately contact us or they provide aggregate referral information.

Retention and your choices

Contact records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the conversation, legal obligations and security. You may ask to access, correct or delete information connected to your contact by emailing the address above. Applicable law may require or permit limited retention.

Changes

This notice will be updated before INVERT introduces analytics, payments, cloud content sync or other material data flows. The date at the top reflects the latest public version.
