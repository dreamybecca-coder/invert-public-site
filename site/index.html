The thesis

A decision system built for you.

AI can generate more analysis than any investor can read. The scarce advantage is a system that knows what matters, challenges what you believe, remembers what you decided, and improves the method after the outcome.

INVERT is building that system for people who already invest and genuinely want to become better investors.
