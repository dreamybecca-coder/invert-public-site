Capabilities, not a shelf of prompts.

INVERT turns repeated investment research jobs into governed capabilities. Each one must be easy to discover, possible to verify, and predictable to call before it becomes a public tool.

01

The external product unit is a Capability.

Skills and methods remain internal ingredients. A Capability combines only the methods needed for one recognizable user job and adds the evidence, evaluation, rights and operating boundaries required for external use.

Internal

Skills & methods

Raw analytical procedures, adapters and checklists.

↓
Published unit

Capability

A stable job, contract, evidence model and failure boundary.

↓

02

Three public contracts, released in order.

A planned capability can be discoverable before it is callable, but its status must remain explicit.

01

Discovery

Stable identity, user job, use cases, exclusions, markets, language, status and canonical URL.

Who should find it, and when?
02

Trust & Evidence

Methodology, provenance, freshness, coverage, licenses, evaluations, limitations and change history.

Why should anyone rely on it?
03

Invocation

Schemas, interfaces, authentication, pricing, errors, latency, privacy, retention, examples and support.

Can an agent call it predictably?
