Account data

Access, correction and deletion

These instructions apply to invited INVERT test accounts. Registration and sign-in begin in the INVERT App, not on the public website.

Updated August 1, 2026

Make a request

Email privacy@invertagent.com from the address connected to the invited account. State whether you want access, correction or deletion.

Verification

We may need to verify that the requester controls the account email before changing or disclosing account data. Do not send passwords, verification codes or brokerage credentials.

What the request covers

A request may cover the remote account identifier, verified email, optional unverified phone number, invitation redemption, sessions and related security or consent records. Content stored only on your device must be exported or deleted on that device. Data sent directly to a third-party model provider is subject to that provider’s terms and controls.

Local data is separate

Signing in is not cloud backup. A new device does not automatically recover Room content, Vault files, memory or model-provider keys from a previous device.

Limits

Some records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, dispute prevention or legal obligations. We will explain any material limitation that applies to a confirmed request.
