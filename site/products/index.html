02Public dashboard

13F Consensus Dashboard

Turn delayed institutional filings into an inspectable map of consensus and change.

Live now. Compare 13 selected managers across five quarterly filings, inspect concentration, overlap and position changes, with ARK weekly activity kept as a separate high-frequency context.

Use it for
Finding recurring ownership, comparing quarter-over-quarter conviction and deciding what deserves deeper research.
Research boundary
13F filings are delayed and cover reported U.S. long positions. This is not a real-time signal or a complete portfolio view.
Availability
Public, read-only and free to open. The current dashboard interface is in Chinese.

03Public dashboard

Macro Market Sentiment Monitor

Put credit, volatility, breadth, liquidity and macro leading indicators into one inspectable view of the market's risk environment.

Live now. Read U.S. equity risk appetite in layers: a sentiment spectrum combines high-yield spreads, volatility, options positioning, breadth and momentum; real rates and net liquidity calibrate pressure on long-duration assets; the yield curve and initial claims provide the slower macro backdrop.

Use it for
Checking whether the market is in a fear, neutral or greed regime, spotting a deterioration in risk conditions and deciding when to hold, wait or investigate further.
Data cadence
FRED and Yahoo Finance inputs refresh on trading days. Manually maintained inputs refresh weekly, and the page exposes timestamps and degraded sources.
Research boundary
The layers operate on different time horizons. The composite is a research-navigation aid, not a real-time quote, short-term timing model or personalized investment advice.
Availability
Public, read-only and free to open. The current dashboard interface is in Chinese.
