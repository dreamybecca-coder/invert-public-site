About INVERT

We do not replace decisions. We improve decision quality.

INVERT is a research and product brand built around one question: how can people become better investors with AI without surrendering judgment or responsibility?

Mission

Help every investor willing to take responsibility build and evolve a judgment system with AI.

Vision

Make professional, continuously improving investment capability accessible beyond institutions and full-time researchers.

North star

Make every investment decision improve the next one.

Built through practice

The product begins where real investment work breaks.

INVERT is being shaped through a live Codex + Obsidian investment system, focused products, an internal App alpha and repeated research audits. The purpose is not to reproduce generic AI tooling. It is to discover and solve the investment-specific gaps that remain.

Current stage

Private alpha, public journal and open-source experiments.

Research disciplines

Principles only matter when they constrain the work.

These disciplines guide both public research and the products INVERT may publish.

01

Point-in-time truth

Keep the source, timestamp, unit, currency and data definition attached. Historical research must not borrow information that was unavailable at the time.

02

Data quality before conclusions

Establish source priority and coverage. Conflicts remain visible; missing data never becomes a confident fact by convenience.

03

A strategy must name its domain

Every method states its objective, market, industry, horizon, costs, constraints and failure conditions. There is no universal score for every context.

04

Facts, inferences and scenarios stay separate

A cited source does not make an interpretation true. We label what is known, inferred, assumed and still missing.

05

Seek disconfirming evidence

Every thesis records alternatives, invalidation conditions and the variables that would force a change of mind.

06

Risk before return

Permanent loss, liquidity, concentration, valuation, execution cost and scenario asymmetry are examined before potential upside.

07

Separate process from luck

The pre-decision record and the retrospective remain distinct. A profit can follow weak reasoning; a loss can follow a sound process.

08

Corrections remain traceable

Data errors, reasoning failures and method changes keep a versioned record. Experience enters a durable method only after evidence and human review.
